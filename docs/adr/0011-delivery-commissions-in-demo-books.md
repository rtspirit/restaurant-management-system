# 0011: Delivery commissions are in the demo books

Date: 2026-11-21. Status: decided by Rahul.

- **Decision.** What the delivery apps keep from each order is a cost in the books, so the profit and loss no longer shows more profit than there is. It is a running cost below prime cost: it lowers net profit and nothing else. Net sales, food and drink cost, staff cost and prime cost do not move.
- **How it is recorded.** No new model, action or field. Each weekly payout statement also becomes one statement from the delivery company (a `vendor_bill` from the vendor Ferrymeal or Trolleybite, dated the last day of the week the orders were sold), with one line for each thing the app kept (commission, sponsored listing, refund charge), coded to account 7500 "Delivery platform commissions and fees" and posted with `post_vendor_bill`. Every statement is recorded as paid, on the day of its payout: the app has already kept the money, so a statement is never a bill to pay and never overdue. That account was already in every restaurant's chart (`seed_chart_of_accounts`, section `controllable`), so no restaurant needs a new one. `weekly_pnl` shows it as its own line and takes it off net profit. `delivery_payout_reconciliation` gains one column, `kept_by_platform`, which is the same number.
- **Where the numbers come from.** Nothing new is generated. The amounts are read off the payout statements the seed already makes from each day's delivery orders (`packages/seed/src/delivery.ts`), so the books, the payout check and the till agree to the cent. Delivery is about 6% of Marigold Kitchen's sales and about 32% of Slice Yard's. Commission is 20% to 25% of those orders, and with fees the apps keep about 26%.
- **Numbers.** Marigold Kitchen, week of 2026-11-09: $1,306.56 kept (commission $1,205.95), net profit $29,885.45, was $31,192.01. Slow week of 2026-10-19: $1,003.49, net profit $19,881.99. Other weeks $1,271.53 to $1,312.63. Slice Yard, week of 2026-11-09: $2,369.25 kept, net profit $9,396.23, was $11,765.48. Slow week of 2026-10-05: $1,681.92. Other weeks $2,163.83 to $2,408.16.
- **Known simplification.** A bill credits "Bills owed to vendors", although the app has already taken the money out of the payout. So the balance sheet shows the full payout as due from the app and the same amount as owed to it, instead of one net figure. A later `post_delivery_payout` action should net the two. A list of supplier bills now also holds the delivery statements (32 for Marigold, 16 for Slice Yard over the eight weeks). The sales sync still reports the 128 bills the Demo POS sent.
